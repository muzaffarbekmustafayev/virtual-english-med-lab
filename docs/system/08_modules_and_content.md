## 2. Modul Strukturasining Tafsilotlari (Modul 1 Misolida)

### Modul 1: Dental Pain & Sensitivity

#### A. Patient Context (AI System Prompt)
> "Patient is a 30-year-old adult presenting with sharp, short pain in the upper right molar when drinking cold water or eating ice cream. No nocturnal pain. The patient is afraid that they might need an extraction."

#### B. Vocabulary (Tibbiy Lug'at)
1. **Hypersensitivity** — Yuqori ta'sirchanlik (`Extreme responsiveness to hot or cold stimuli`)
2. **Dentin** — Dentin qavati (`Calcified tissue beneath enamel`)
3. **Enamel** — Tish emali (`Hard white outer surface of tooth`)
4. **Trigger** — Qo'zg'atuvchi omil (`Factor causing symptom onset`)
5. **Reversible Pulpitis** — Qaytar pulpit (`Mild inflammation of dental pulp`)

#### C. Smart Phrasebook Kategoriyalari
1. **Asking about pain onset & duration:**
   - *"When did you first notice this sensitivity?"*
   - *"How long does the sharp pain last after drinking cold water?"*
2. **Inquiring about triggers:**
   - *"Does hot food or sweet drinks also trigger the pain?"*
   - *"Is the pain triggered by breathing cold air?"*
3. **Reassuring the patient:**
   - *"Don't worry, we will do a thorough exam to fix this sensitivity."*

#### D. Module Quiz (Test Savollari Namunasi)
- **Question 1:** Which term best describes brief sharp pain triggered by cold liquids that subsides immediately?
  - A) Irreversible Pulpitis
  - B) **Dentin Hypersensitivity** (To'g'ri)
  - C) Dental Abscess
  - D) Periodontal Pocket
